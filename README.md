# 🛡️ Phishing Email Detection & Awareness System

> A cybersecurity awareness and phishing email analysis project completed as part of the **Future Interns Cyber Security Internship Program**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Phishing remains one of the most common cyber attacks targeting individuals and organizations. Attackers impersonate trusted brands to trick users into revealing sensitive information such as usernames, passwords, banking details, or OTPs.

This project focuses on analyzing real-world phishing email samples, identifying phishing indicators, classifying email risks, and creating an employee awareness guide to help users recognize and prevent phishing attacks.

The objective is to simulate the responsibilities of a Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst by performing structured phishing email investigations and documenting the findings in a professional report.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze phishing email samples
- Identify common phishing indicators
- Classify the risk level of each email
- Understand email authentication mechanisms (SPF, DKIM, and DMARC)
- Create employee awareness and prevention guidelines
- Produce a professional phishing analysis report

## 🔍 Analysis Methodology

Each phishing email was analyzed using a structured approach:

1. Review the sender information
2. Examine the subject line
3. Inspect the email body
4. Identify social engineering techniques
5. Analyze links (where applicable)
6. Review email authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) when available
7. Identify phishing indicators
8. Classify the overall risk level
9. Document findings

---

## 🚩 Phishing Indicators Identified

During the investigation, the following phishing indicators were observed:

- Brand impersonation
- Suspicious sender email addresses
- Generic greetings
- Urgent or fear-based language
- Credential harvesting attempts
- Suspicious hyperlinks
- Financial reward lures
- Requests for sensitive information
- Missing or failed email authentication
- Poor grammar and formatting

---

## 📊 Email Risk Classification

| Risk Level | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| 🟢 Safe | No phishing indicators detected |
| 🟡 Suspicious | Some suspicious characteristics present |
| 🔴 High Risk Phishing | Multiple phishing indicators identified and immediate caution required |
